# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, safe, and constructive community. Everyone
participating in this project is expected to act with respect, empathy, and
professionalism regardless of experience, identity, background, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code on their merits.
- Give specific, actionable feedback and assume good intent.
- Respect boundaries, privacy, and requests to stop unwelcome behavior.
- Keep disagreements focused on the project.
- Help make participation accessible to newcomers.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, sustained
disruption, and publishing another person's private information are not
acceptable. Retaliation against someone who raises a good-faith concern is also
not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in project repositories, issue trackers, reviews, release
channels, and public events where someone represents the project.

For an immediate safety risk, contact the relevant platform or local emergency
service. Otherwise, report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer
contact associated with the `anhedral` npm package. Do not open a public issue
containing sensitive personal information.

Maintainers will acknowledge reports, protect confidentiality as far as
practical, investigate fairly, and take proportionate action. Responses may
include a private warning, content removal, temporary participation limits, or
a permanent ban. Maintainers with a conflict of interest must recuse themselves.

Good-faith reports will not result in retaliation. Knowingly false or malicious
reports may themselves violate this policy.
